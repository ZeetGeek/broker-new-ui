Compact desktop sizes remain available; no overlapping invisible hit areas were added. Updated size-reference notes and design guidance. Android spacing/overflow and physical-device hit testing remain unverified.

All changes are frontend-only. Contrast was recalculated with the sRGB relative-luminance formula in PowerShell. ## Verdict

**The owner marketplace is partly hidden, but the owner side is not fully disabled. The broker portal also has several functional problems that should be fixed before launch.**

The main broker navigation, marketplace dashboard cards, and the `/broker/owner-listings` and `/broker/my-deals` layouts respect the feature flag. However, email signup still defaults to Owner, Google onboarding still offers Owner, and `/owner/*` has no launch-feature gate. Saved properties, legacy request URLs, owner profiles, notifications, and referrals provide additional owner-linked entry points.

The most serious functional problem is false success: logging a visit outcome updates the screen without saving anything to the API. Dashboard follow-up completion is also a local simulation. Other significant problems include notes changing finished deals back to Contacted, buyer attachment loading only owner-approved inventory, and visit bookings displaying more buyers than they actually submit.

**Recommendation: hold the broker-only release until the P0 issue and the applicable P1 items below are resolved and the live acceptance checks pass.** Passing compilation and unit tests does not establish that the broker workflow works end to end.

## Launch scope used for this review

Your latest instructions take precedence over the older owner-first core-loop descriptions in `AGENTS.md` and `PRODUCT.md`:

- Launch for brokers first. Brokers enter their own property inventory and manage buyers, owners, deals, and visits.
- Keep owner accounts, owner marketplace discovery, representation requests, owner invitations, and owner-approval workflows out of this launch.
- Preserve the owner-side implementation for a later phase; hiding it does not require deleting its code or data.
- **Keep broker-added owner contacts.** An offline owner recorded by a broker is different from an owner with a platform account.
- Keep public property sharing without buyer accounts. Call and ordinary WhatsApp links remain useful broker actions.
- Do not introduce new roles, finalize pipeline stages, decide pricing, or invent a referral reward policy as part of cleanup.
