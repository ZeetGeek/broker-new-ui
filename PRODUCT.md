# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Property owners, builders, independent brokers, and broker organizations in Surat. They are often non-technical, work primarily on inexpensive Android phones, and need fast, clear workflows over ordinary Indian mobile connections.

## Product Purpose

Connect owner property supply directly to a broker CRM through a consent-gated representation workflow. A property is entered once by its owner, approved for representation, and then flows into the broker pipeline without manual re-entry. Success means both sides can understand and complete this loop confidently on a phone.

The current product name is a placeholder and must remain isolated in configuration until final branding is decided.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, restrained. The interface should feel warm and capable without looking ornamental, overly soft, or consumer-toy-like.

## Anti-references

- iOS-style squircles, oversized corner smoothing, and pill-shaped containers used as a default visual language
- Generic shadcn scaffolds with cool gray surfaces or unmodified component styling
- Decorative gradients, glass effects, excessive shadows, and color used without meaning
- Desktop-first CRM layouts compressed onto a phone
- Technical or internal terminology exposed to users when plain language is available

## Design Principles

1. Make the consent decision unmistakable. Owners should understand who wants to represent which property and what approval does.
2. Enter property data once. No interaction should force a broker to retype information already supplied by an owner.
3. Compose for a 360px screen first. Desktop layouts are adaptations of a clear mobile priority order.
4. Earn trust through hierarchy, plain language, and restrained visual treatment.
5. Use one consistent design-token system so shared primitives, feature components, and future screens stay aligned.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain at least AA text contrast, visible keyboard focus, non-color status cues, reduced-motion support, readable tap targets, and resilient layouts at 360px. Copy should remain short, concrete, and understandable to people reading English as a second or third language.
